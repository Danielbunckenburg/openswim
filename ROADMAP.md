# Roadmap

Milestones are intentions, not release promises.

## 0 — Foundation
Repository, documentation, CI, website, workout domain model.

## 1 — Wear OS prototype
Local workout library and detail, pool selection, active instructions, manual repetition progression, rest timer, pause/resume, completion. Current focus.

## 2 — Real swim tracking
Health Services exercise session, supported heart rate, distance, lap and stroke counts, and pool testing.

## 3 — Local history
Persist completed workouts and view performance details.

## 4 — Android companion
Workout builder, planning, history, and watch synchronization.

## 5 — Accounts and cloud sync
Backend, database, authentication, account synchronization.

## 6 — Web planner
Create workouts in a browser and synchronize them to wearables.

## 7 — Apple ecosystem
Apple Watch, iPhone, and HealthKit while retaining shared workout concepts.
