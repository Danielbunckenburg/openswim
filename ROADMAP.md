# Roadmap

OpenSwim develops one issue at a time. These are directions, not release promises or dates. See GitHub Issues for the current backlog.

## 1. Standalone watch app — current priority

Build a complete, clear local manual swimming experience before starting sensor or cloud features. The app now has 12 curated plans, category navigation, compatible pool selection, manual repetition and rest progression, pause/resume, completion, and local flow recovery. Round emulator checks at 454 × 454 and 390 × 390 pixels are complete. Physical pool usability and accessibility review remain.

## 2. Workout player

The local manual player is available. Keep refining progression, touch targets, and edge cases using watch and pool feedback before adding automatic tracking.

## 3. Real swim tracking

Research and prototype Wear OS Health Services exercise sessions, supported distance, heart rate, lap and stroke metrics, and physical pool testing.

## 4. Workout history

Persist completed workouts locally and show session details and basic statistics.

## 5. Android companion

Explore a phone workout builder, planning, history, and communication with the watch.

## 6. Accounts and synchronization

Consider authentication, an API, storage, and synchronization when the local experience is proven.

## 7. Web application

Explore browser-based workout planning, calendar, history, and library.

## 8. Community

Possible ideas include shared workouts, workout links, training plans, and a community library.

## 9. Apple ecosystem

Explore Apple Watch, iPhone, and HealthKit after the core watch experience is established.
